THE DETAILS

Before you
jump in.

Will it increase my FPS or lower ping?

Results depend on your hardware, game, driver, and workload. No fixed improvement is promised. Network tests measure ICMP round-trip time to your chosen IP, not Roblox server ping or mouse-to-screen latency. Compare one change at a time.

What games does it support?

Windows-level controls apply system-wide. Roblox has an optional desktop-player priority control and dedicated guidance. Fortnite, VALORANT, CS2, and Minecraft have guidance-only profiles. Game compatibility has not been benchmark-certified.

Can I restore my changes?

Yes. Automatic changes save their original values for restore. Settings you change manually in Windows or in a game are not tracked by Kryptic. Keep the local restore file until you have restored your changes.

Does it need Python or a subscription?

The executable includes its runtime. This preview has no payment processing or subscription service. Its shared password is local access control, not a paid license.

What data leaves my PC?

The app has no telemetry uploader or account service. Restore data, activity, and diagnostic reports are local. When you run a network measurement, it sends ten ICMP probes to the IP address you choose. This website has no analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

How do I remove it?

Restore any tweaks you want undone, close Kryptic, then remove its installation folder and shortcuts. The installation folder is under your local app data, Programs/KrypticsTweaks. Your local restore data remains until you remove it.
